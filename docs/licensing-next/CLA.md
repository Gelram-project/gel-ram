## 12. Acceptance process

Before opening a pull request intended for merge, an external Contributor must contact `gelram.licensing@gmail.com` and complete this Agreement privately.

This Agreement becomes effective only when the Contributor has signed or otherwise accepted it in a durable written or electronic record and the Project Licensor has countersigned it or sent a durable written or electronic acceptance identifying the Contributor and the applicable CLA version.

The Project Licensor must maintain a private record sufficient to show the Contributor, CLA version, effective date, acceptance, and any agreed coverage of earlier Contributions.

A pull request acknowledgement or CI checkbox is only a declaration by the Contributor. It is not a substitute for the Project Licensor's private record that the completed Agreement is on file.

A Contribution submitted without a completed applicable CLA may be closed, rejected, or left unmerged.

This document is the complete contributor license agreement for Contributions covered by GEL RAM CLA 2.0. Amendments must be in writing and accepted by both the Contributor and the Project Licensor.
