# GEL RAM CLA privacy notice

This notice describes the handling of personal information collected to administer GEL RAM Contributor License Agreement 2.0.

## 1. Controller and contact

The project-side controller for CLA records is the Project Licensor identified in the operative CLA as RR — GEL RAM Project, or its valid legal successor.

Privacy and licensing contact: `gelram.licensing@gmail.com`.

RR is a public pseudonym, not a registered-company claim. Initial enquiries use the project email. Before requesting completed agreements or authority records, the Project Licensor must provide the controller information and other notices required for the actual processing. Private email alone does not satisfy every possible transparency requirement. Do not request identity documents in a public repository.

## 2. Data collected

CLA administration may collect:

1. contributor legal name;
2. email address;
3. organization and title or signing authority, when applicable;
4. GitHub username, when supplied;
5. signature or equivalent electronic acceptance record;
6. effective and acceptance dates;
7. correspondence needed to verify authority, contribution provenance, or CLA status;
8. identifiers of contributions covered by the agreement when needed for chain-of-rights records;
9. the exact accepted agreement version or digest and proportionate evidence of organizational signing authority, where relevant.

Do not send government identity documents, home addresses, payment-card data, passwords, private keys, health information, or other unnecessary sensitive information unless the Project Licensor specifically and lawfully requires it for a documented reason.

Submitting a contribution and seeking a CLA are voluntary. Information necessary to establish identity, authority and durable acceptance is needed to accept a contribution under the CLA; without it, the Project Licensor may be unable to accept that contribution. A GitHub username is optional unless needed to associate a submission with its agreement. Ordinary permitted use of the Software or publication of a fork does not by itself require a CLA or submission of these personal records.

## 3. Purposes

CLA information is processed only as reasonably necessary to establish and maintain evidence of contributor permissions; determine whether a contribution may be accepted and relicensed; maintain copyright, patent, provenance and chain-of-rights records; contact the contributor about the agreement or covered contributions; investigate or respond to licensing, ownership, security or legal disputes; comply with legal obligations; and protect or enforce project rights.

CLA information is not collected for advertising or sale as a personal-data product.

## 4. Intended legal basis where UK GDPR applies

Where the UK GDPR applies, the intended bases are the Project Licensor's legitimate interests in maintaining reliable contribution and intellectual-property records and, where applicable, processing necessary to take steps requested by the contributor or to administer the contributor agreement.

The appropriate lawful basis must match the actual controller, storage process and jurisdiction. This notice does not claim that a particular legal basis applies where the relevant data-protection law differs.

## 5. Storage and access

Completed CLAs and related evidence must be kept privately. They must not be committed to the public repository, attached to public issues, or posted in pull requests.

Access should be limited to the Project Licensor and service providers or professional advisers that reasonably need access for the purposes above and are subject to appropriate confidentiality or data-protection obligations.

## 6. Retention

CLA records may be retained for as long as GEL RAM distributes, licenses, or relies on contributions covered by the agreement and afterwards for any period reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, defend, or document legal rights and applicable limitation periods.

Records that are no longer reasonably necessary for those purposes should be deleted or anonymized where legally and technically appropriate.

## 7. Service providers and international processing

Email, document-storage, hosting, backup, legal, or security providers may process CLA information on behalf of or in support of the Project Licensor. The exact providers and any international-transfer safeguards must be maintained consistently with applicable data-protection law.

## 8. Individual rights

Depending on applicable law, a contributor may have rights to request access, correction, restriction, objection, portability, or deletion of personal information. Some requests may be limited where retention is necessary to preserve evidence of a license grant, comply with law, or establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Requests should be sent to `gelram.licensing@gmail.com`.

Where UK data-protection law applies, an individual may also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office or another competent supervisory authority.

### Right to object

Where processing relies on legitimate interests and applicable law provides that right, You may object on grounds relating to Your particular situation by writing to `gelram.licensing@gmail.com`. The Project Licensor must assess the objection under applicable law; an objection does not automatically erase records necessary for legal claims or revoke an effective copyright or patent license.

### Automated decisions

The CLA acceptance process is not intended to be a solely automated decision. CI acknowledgement checks do not establish identity, authority or a legally effective agreement. If actual processing later includes profiling or solely automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects, the Project Licensor must provide information required by applicable law.

## 9. Security

The Project Licensor should use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of CLA records, including restricted access and protected storage. No public document creates a guarantee that any system is immune from compromise.

## 10. Changes

Material changes to CLA data handling should be reflected in an updated notice. A later notice does not retroactively change the copyright or patent rights granted by an already effective CLA.
