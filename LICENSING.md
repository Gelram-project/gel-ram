This repository distribution uses **GEL RAM Noncommercial Reciprocal License 1.0 (GEL RAM NCRL 1.0)** for GEL RAM-owned material. The controlling public-license text is [LICENSE](LICENSE).

GEL RAM NCRL 1.0 is source-available and noncommercial. It is not represented as an OSI-approved open-source license.

## 1. Core rule

1. If You do not monetize GEL RAM, You may use it, study it, modify it, keep private changes private, and share it for noncommercial purposes.
2. Anyone, including a company, may test, benchmark, prototype, integrate, and modify GEL RAM for Evaluation without a fixed time limit, provided the work remains non-production and does not operate or materially improve a monetized product or service.
3. If any individual, freelancer, sole trader, company, nonprofit, or other organization wants to earn money or obtain material financial benefit from GEL RAM or a GEL RAM-derived Modification, commercial rights must be obtained before that Monetized Use begins.
4. Creating Your own GEL RAM-derived modifications does not avoid the commercial requirement.
5. Commercial terms may use a reciprocal model, where payment and agreed improvements are shared, or a closed model, where specifically authorized modifications may remain private in exchange for separately negotiated compensation.

## 2. Noncommercial use and sharing

Permitted noncommercial examples include personal study, hobby projects, independent noncommercial research, classroom teaching, public-interest work that is not serving a commercial beneficiary, private collaboration, public reproducibility and benchmarking, and noncommercial sharing of unmodified or modified GEL RAM.

Private noncommercial modifications may remain private without a time limit. There is no automatic source-publication deadline merely because a person has modified or used GEL RAM privately.

Recipients of shared GEL RAM remain subject to the public license and do not receive commercial rights.

The same noncommercial rule applies to project-owned demonstration films and screenshots identified in [media/RIGHTS.md](media/RIGHTS.md): they may be viewed, copied, and shared noncommercially under the root license. Monetized reuse requires a Commercial Agreement.

## 3. Free Evaluation for everyone

Evaluation is available to any person or organization, including for-profit companies, without a fixed 30-day clock.

Evaluation may include source review, benchmarking, tests, prototypes, integration experiments, compatibility work, and temporary modifications in a non-production environment.

Evaluation does not permit customer-facing production deployment, paid or subscription services, advertising-supported production operation, billable client delivery, production training or tuning, deliverables for a paying customer, revenue-generating infrastructure, or production use that materially improves or reduces the operating cost of a monetized service.

A company may therefore test freely, but it must obtain commercial rights before the test becomes production or Monetized Use.

## 4. What counts as monetization

A separate Commercial Agreement is required when GEL RAM or a GEL RAM-derived Modification is used in connection with a paid product, SaaS or hosted service, subscription or paid access, advertising revenue, sponsorship tied to access or operation, paid consulting or contract work, customer-facing production systems, revenue-generating infrastructure, commercial deployment, or production use that materially reduces the cost of a commercial activity.

This rule applies to everyone. The distinction is **noncommercial versus monetized use**, not **company versus private person**.

Donations, tips, crowdfunding, sponsorships, or similar payments received specifically because of a GEL RAM-based activity are treated as monetization when they fund, reward, or support that activity. An unrelated personal gift is outside this rule.

## 5. Commercial options

[COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md](COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md) describes the commercial negotiation model. It is not itself a commercial grant.

### Reciprocal commercial option

The commercial user pays the agreed subscription, fee, royalty, revenue share, or other compensation and shares the agreed GEL RAM-derived Commercial Improvements or Corresponding Source as specified in the signed agreement.

### Closed-modification commercial option

The commercial user may keep specifically authorized GEL RAM-derived improvements private only when a signed Commercial Agreement expressly grants that right. This option may require a higher subscription, fee, royalty, revenue share, minimum payment, or other compensation.

Payment by itself does not create commercial rights. Closed rights do not arise from an unsolicited payment. The signed Commercial Agreement controls.

## 6. Updates, extensions and subscriptions

A Commercial Agreement may tie access to future versions, extensions, modules, updates, support, security fixes, private branches, or closed-modification rights to an active subscription or continuing payment.

Exact prices, revenue-share percentages, update rights, termination effects and support terms belong in the signed Commercial Agreement, not in the public license.

## 7. Independent components

An independently developed component does not become a GEL RAM Modification merely because it communicates through an API, protocol, file format, or process boundary.

Copied or adapted GEL RAM code does not become independent merely because it is moved into a wrapper, plugin, generated file, encoded payload, or separate process.

## 9. Contributions and CLA privacy

External code contributions intended for merge require **GEL RAM CLA 2.0** from [CLA.md](CLA.md).

The CLA becomes effective only after both Contributor acceptance and Project Licensor written acceptance are recorded privately. A CI checkbox is not proof of a completed CLA.

The CLA is non-exclusive. Contributors retain ownership of their contributions while granting the Project Licensor rights sufficient to maintain the public license and offer separate commercial licenses.

Personal information collected for CLA administration is covered by [CLA-PRIVACY.md](CLA-PRIVACY.md) and must not be published in the repository.
