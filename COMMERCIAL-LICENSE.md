The public GEL RAM Noncommercial Reciprocal License 1.0 allows noncommercial use and free Evaluation. It does not grant Monetized Use.

Commercial rights arise only from a separate written agreement signed by the Project Licensor and the commercial licensee. This applies equally to an individual, freelancer, sole trader, company, nonprofit, or other organization that wants to monetize GEL RAM or a GEL RAM-derived Modification.

## 1. Reciprocal commercial

A reciprocal commercial agreement may allow Monetized Use in exchange for:

1. an agreed subscription, fixed fee, usage fee, royalty, revenue share, or other payment; and
2. delivery or publication of agreed GEL RAM-derived Commercial Improvements or their Corresponding Source on the terms stated in the agreement.

This is the intended lower-cost route when the commercial user is willing to share agreed improvements back.

## 2. Closed commercial

A closed commercial agreement may expressly permit specified GEL RAM-derived Commercial Improvements to remain private.

Closed commercial rights are not automatic. They may require a higher subscription tier, larger fixed fee, higher royalty or revenue share, minimum payment, or another separately negotiated form of compensation.

A payment made without a signed agreement does not create closed rights or any other commercial permission.

## 3. Updates and extensions

A commercial agreement may condition access to future versions, extensions, modules, updates, support, security fixes, private branches, or closed-modification rights on an active subscription or continuing payment.

The agreement should state clearly what happens when payment stops, including which previously supplied versions may continue to be used, whether production rights survive, what improvement-sharing duties continue, and whether future updates or extensions stop.

## 4. Terms that must be explicit

A commercial agreement should identify, as applicable:

1. the actual legal parties and signing authority;
2. covered GEL RAM versions, commits, archives, products, services, systems, teams, affiliates, and deployment environments;
3. whether the agreement uses the reciprocal or closed-modification model;
4. the exact payment model and due dates;
5. any royalty or revenue-share base and exclusions;
6. the Commercial Improvements that must be shared, if any;
7. the Commercial Improvements that may remain closed, if any;
8. internal business use, production use, redistribution, embedding, contractor access, and sublicensing rights;
9. subscription term, renewal, cancellation, and post-termination rights;
10. access to updates, extensions, modules, support, or private branches;
11. confidentiality, security, audit scope, and reporting where applicable;
12. warranties, indemnities, liability allocation, governing law, and dispute resolution if agreed.

No price, discount, royalty rate, revenue-share percentage, support promise, service level, warranty, indemnity, exclusivity, or other commercial term is created by this notice.

Publishing source does not grant commercial rights. Keeping source private does not become lawful merely because the user pays something. The signed Commercial Agreement controls both payment and modification-sharing obligations.
