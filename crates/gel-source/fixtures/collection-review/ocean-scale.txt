# Ocean Scale: scope and limits of a withdrawn research bundle

This page described a numerical research bundle that extended the public v0.3.0
line. The owner withdrew the bundle on 2026-09-27; it is not part of this
repository and is not supported. The page is kept only as a frozen search
document for the collection review.

## What the bundle covered

- Full ranked scans and evidence checks on 1M/10M numerical campaigns.
- A read-only check of a kernel-sealed copy, not zero-copy disk I/O.
- Raw measurements and English results and limitations.

No private chat engine, private encoder, private knowledge bank, user data,
keys or private network implementation was included.

## Measured scope and limits

EXACT full scan → top10 → decision, milliseconds, 24 workers:

| ORB count | Seed | N | p50 | p95 | p99 | max |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1M | 41119 | 100 | 72.427 | 81.018 | 82.859 | 90.949 |
| 1M | 61141 | 100 | 71.962 | 79.860 | 80.576 | 88.227 |
| 10M | 41119 | 100 | 615.428 | 684.888 | 710.313 | 734.085 |
| 10M | 61141 | 100 | 704.995 | 757.900 | 783.038 | 810.236 |

A shorter campaign had a faster 10M/24 median of 498.265 ms. It is not
substituted for the longer runs. It compares 1/12/24 workers with the same
queries; its median paired 1→24 speedup is 9.425× at 10M, not 24×.
Do not interpret these full scans as addressed-read latency or general AI recall.
100 observations do not establish stable p99.9–p99.999 tails.

A longer repeat run included 2,800 operations, 660M bitwise score comparisons and 177,600 scalar
oracle checks, all matching expectations. Synthetic EXACT/NEAR/PARTIAL/MISS/
WEAK/TIE/EMPTY are not natural-language evaluation classes. TIE repeats one
distinct query; EMPTY does not scan the full bank.

Host: 24 logical CPUs, Linux, Rust 1.85.0. A live desktop could run in the
background. GPU inference, cold-cache behaviour, semantic truth, hardware-level
memory computation and hardware identity are not established.
SIGKILL tests do not prove survival of physical power loss. Hashes are not
signatures; storage requires a separately trusted owner-controlled anchor.
